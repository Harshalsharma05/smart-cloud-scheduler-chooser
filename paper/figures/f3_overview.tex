\caption{System overview. At an event the controller reads the target and, from a prediction file, each running job's remaining-runtime quantiles and failure and cancellation probabilities, then sets a cluster-wide cap level and suspends or bursts jobs. Solid arrows: signals and actions; dashed: data (predictions, checkpoints); dotted: facility power.}
