\paragraph{Carbon side check.}
Carbon is a side check, not the main objective. We use annual average emission rates from the eGRID2023 summary tables\footnote{US EPA, eGRID2023 Summary Tables (rev.\ 1), Table 1, total CO$_2$ output emission rate, \url{https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2025-01/egrid2023_summary_tables_rev1.pdf}, accessed 15 November 2026.}: \EcarbonLocal{} g/kWh where the events occur and \EcarbonCloudEastOne{}--\EcarbonCloudEastTwo{} g/kWh for three cloud regions. They are average, not marginal, rates, and we count only energy inside the event window; energy that delay or capping postpones to later is not counted. Table~\ref{tab:carbon} gives cloud emissions as a percentage of the emissions avoided on site, pooled over \EcarbonEvents{} feasible events. Delay and mix-nocloud add none. Mix with the transformer's p90 estimate adds \EcarbonMixEastTwoAddedPct\% in the highest-intensity region, and mix with the oracle \EcarbonOracleEastOneAddedPct\% to \EcarbonOracleEastTwoAddedPct\%, depending on the region.
