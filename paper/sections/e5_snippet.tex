\paragraph{Carbon side check.}
Carbon is a side check, not the main objective. We use annual average emission rates from the eGRID2023 summary tables\footnote{US EPA, eGRID2023 Summary Tables (rev.\ 1), Table 1, total CO$_2$ output emission rate, \url{https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2025-01/egrid2023_summary_tables_rev1.pdf}, accessed 15 November 2026.}: \EcarbonLocal{} g/kWh for the grid where events occur and 268--413 g/kWh for three cloud regions. These are average, not marginal, rates. We count only energy inside the event window; energy that delay or capping postpones to later is not counted. Table~\ref{tab:carbon} gives the median net kg CO$_2$ per MWh shed over \EcarbonEvents{} feasible events. Delay and mix-nocloud avoid \EcarbonDelayWestNet{}. Mix with the transformer (p90) gives \EcarbonMixEastTwoNet{}--\EcarbonMixWestNet{}, and mix with the oracle \EcarbonOracleEastTwoNet{}--\EcarbonOracleWestNet{}. Net emissions are positive in \EcarbonOracleWestShare\% of events for every policy and region; cloud emissions are \EcarbonMixAddedPct\% (transformer) and \EcarbonOracleAddedPct\% (oracle) of avoided emissions in the highest-intensity region.
